# Common Plant Issues — Quick Reference

Visual signatures for the most frequently diagnosed problems. Use these patterns
to match symptoms in a plant photo to a likely cause.

## Diseases

| Disease              | Visual Signature                                                       | Common Hosts           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Early Blight         | Concentric brown rings ("target spots") on lower leaves, yellow halo   | Tomato, Potato         |
| Late Blight          | Water-soaked dark patches, white fuzzy growth on underside              | Tomato, Potato         |
| Powdery Mildew       | White powdery coating on leaf surfaces                                 | Squash, Rose, Grape    |
| Downy Mildew         | Yellow patches on top, gray-purple fuzz underneath                     | Cucumber, Lettuce      |
| Septoria Leaf Spot   | Small dark spots with light gray centers on lower leaves               | Tomato                 |
| Bacterial Wilt       | Sudden wilting despite moist soil, stems ooze when cut                 | Tomato, Cucumber       |
| Fusarium Wilt        | Yellowing on one side of the plant, brown vascular tissue              | Tomato, Banana         |
| Anthracnose          | Sunken dark lesions on fruit, stems, or leaves                         | Mango, Bean, Pepper    |
| Mosaic Virus         | Mottled light/dark green mosaic pattern on leaves, leaf curling        | Tomato, Tobacco, Pepper|
| Rust                 | Orange-brown pustules on leaf undersides                               | Wheat, Bean, Rose      |
| Root Rot             | Wilting, yellowing, mushy brown roots (needs uprooting to confirm)     | Most species           |

## Pests

| Pest                 | Visual Signature                                                       | Damage Pattern         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Aphids               | Clusters of small soft-bodied insects on new growth, sticky honeydew   | Curled new leaves      |
| Whiteflies           | Tiny white flying insects on leaf undersides, sticky residue           | Yellowing leaves       |
| Spider Mites         | Fine webbing on leaf undersides, stippled/bronzed leaves               | Dry, dusty conditions  |
| Thrips               | Silver streaks or scars on leaves, tiny slender insects                | Distorted new growth   |
| Leaf Miners          | Winding white or pale trails inside leaves                             | Tunnels in leaf tissue |
| Caterpillars         | Irregular holes chewed in leaves, frass (droppings) present            | Large missing leaf areas|

## Nutrient Deficiencies

| Deficiency           | Visual Signature                                                       | Appears On             |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Nitrogen (N)         | Uniform yellowing starting from older/lower leaves                     | Old leaves first       |
| Phosphorus (P)       | Purple/reddish tint on leaves, stunted growth                          | Old leaves first       |
| Potassium (K)        | Brown scorching on leaf edges                                          | Old leaves first       |
| Iron (Fe)            | Interveinal chlorosis — veins stay green, tissue between turns yellow  | New leaves first       |
| Magnesium (Mg)       | Interveinal chlorosis on older leaves                                  | Old leaves first       |
| Calcium (Ca)         | Distorted/curled new growth, blossom end rot on fruit                  | New growth, fruit      |

## Environmental Stress

| Stressor             | Visual Signature                                                       | Key Differentiator     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Overwatering         | Yellowing, wilting despite wet soil, edema bumps on leaves             | Soil stays soggy       |
| Underwatering        | Wilting, crispy brown leaf edges, soil pulls away from pot edges       | Soil is bone dry       |
| Sunburn              | Bleached white/tan patches on sun-facing leaf surfaces                 | Only on exposed side   |
| Heat Stress          | Leaf curl, wilting during hottest part of day, recovers at night       | Temporary wilting      |
| Cold Damage          | Water-soaked dark patches, blackened leaf tips                         | After cold event       |
| Transplant Shock     | Wilting, leaf drop shortly after repotting or transplanting            | Recent transplant      |
